Founder of [H2ZTECH](https://h2z.tech), based in the Houston area.

I've spent twenty years building low-voltage and smart-building systems: cloud platforms, hardware, SaaS, mobile apps and access control, delivered on large buildings and campuses.

### What I'm building

**H2Z Riser** is AI that learns low-voltage engineering in the field. It reads the CAD drawing, finds the cameras, recorders and switches already installed, and builds a 3D site the integrator and the customer can both work in. Recordings stay on the customer's own hardware.

### How it's built

H2ZTECH is an AI-native company. I set direction, write the standards and sign off. AI agents do much of the building, research and testing, under written rules in every repository, with a numbered feature list and a test for every feature.

The product lives in private repositories under [@H2ZTech](https://github.com/H2ZTech): recorder core (Go), 3D engine and viewer, site console, AI server, backend, deployment, mobile app, demo environment and website.

### Rules we build by

- AI proposes. People approve.
- Once a step is approved, it runs without a model in the loop.
- Recordings stay on site, not in our cloud.
- If the system estimated something instead of measuring it, it says so.
